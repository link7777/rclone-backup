ams4merch: privacy notice

ams4merch is a private, single-user tool. Its owner uses it to analyse and manage the Amazon Advertising campaigns of their own Amazon Advertising account, through the Amazon Ads API. It is not offered to anyone else, it has no other users, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's authorization through Login with Amazon, the tool reads and writes data for the owner's own advertising account only:

  • Advertising profiles, portfolios, campaigns, ad groups, ads, keywords and targets.
  • Performance reports for those campaigns (impressions, clicks, spend, sales, orders and related metrics), and search-term reports.
  • Campaign, ad group, keyword and target changes the owner asks it to make — bids, budgets, state, and newly created campaigns.

It accesses no data belonging to any other advertiser, and it requests no information about customers, shoppers or any other individual.

What it does with that data

The data is used only to show the owner the state and performance of their own campaigns, and to apply the changes the owner decides on. It is stored in a database file on the owner's own computer and is not published, sold, rented or shared with anyone. Nothing is sent to any third party; the only service the tool talks to is Amazon itself.

Credentials

The Login with Amazon client secret and the access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared.

Retention and withdrawal

Advertising data is kept locally for as long as the owner finds it useful for historical comparison, and can be deleted by the owner at any time by deleting the local database. Authorization can be withdrawn at any time in the owner's Amazon account, after which the tool can no longer read or change anything.
