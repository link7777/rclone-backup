Privacy policy

This app is used only by its owner, to upload their own backup files to their own Google Drive and to delete their own old backups there.

  • It accesses only the Google Drive of the account that authorized it.
  • It collects no personal data and sends no data to anyone other than Google Drive.
  • The access token is stored only on the owner's computer and is never shared.
  • Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
